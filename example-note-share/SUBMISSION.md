# Submission

Keep this file at the top of your task folder, beside `instruction.md`. It goes out with the task, so leave out your name and contact details.

## The task

What is the target, and what must the agent do to succeed? What counts as solved?

## What it tests

Which skill does this measure (the vulnerability class, technique or chain of steps)? What real-world work is it based on?

## How it's solved

What's the intended path, step by step, as `solution/solve.sh` takes it? Which step is the key insight?

## How it's checked

What do the tests check, and why does a wrong or partial answer fail them?

## Why it's hard

Where do you expect a frontier model to go wrong, and why is that a gap in skill rather than unclear instructions or a broken setup?

How long would a skilled practitioner take to solve it?

## What's original

What did you create yourself? List any public sources (CVEs, write-ups, CTFs) you drew on and what you changed.
